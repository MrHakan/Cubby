Tricks

  • Wall climbing — press into a grey wall and keep jumping to scale it.
  • S / N / B orbs — shrink, reset, or grow. Size changes your mass.
  • Gravity orbs — flip your fall, or drag yourself back down.
  • Speed strips — some floors make you fast for the rest of the level.
